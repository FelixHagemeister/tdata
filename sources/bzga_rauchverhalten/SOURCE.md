## BZgA Rauchverhalten der Bevölkerung

Adult smoking behavior survey covering 1997–2021. Complements the
Drogenaffinitätsstudie for the 14+ population.

### PDF reports

Freely available from: <https://www.bzga.de/infomaterialien/alkohol-tabak-drogen/tabak/>

Look for reports titled "Rauchen — Zahlen und Fakten" or similar.

### Microdata

GESIS archival status unconfirmed. Contact: forschung@bzga.de
