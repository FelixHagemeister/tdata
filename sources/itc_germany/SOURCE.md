## ITC Germany Survey

The richest tobacco behavioral dataset for Germany, covering product
use, brand/price, policy attitudes, health perceptions, and cessation
in longitudinal panel design.

### Waves

| Wave | Year | n (Germany) |
|---|---|---|
| ITC 4CV Wave 1 | 2016 | ~4,000 |
| ITC 4CV Wave 2 | 2018 | ~4,000 |
| ITC 4CV Wave 3 | 2020 | ~4,000 |
| ITC Germany Wave 4 | 2022 | ~4,000 |
